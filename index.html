      How RepCord works

      Everything you can do here, explained in plain words. It takes about two minutes to read.

      The idea in 4 steps

      1. Make a split

      A split is one training day, like Push Day. Go to Workouts, then Splits, then New split.

      2. Log your day

      Open Your Calendar, click a day, pick the split and how hard it was, then save.

      3. Update your lifts

      Go to Workouts, then Progress. Type only the weights or reps that changed.

      4. Watch it grow

      Your calendar fills with color, your streak goes up, and your charts show your progress.

      Your calendar is your scoreboard

      Every day is a small square. Train, then color it in. The harder you worked, the brighter the square.

      • LightAn easy day. You had plenty left.
      • ModerateA normal, solid workout.
      • FailureYour hardest days. You pushed until you couldn't do another rep.

      A square with no color is a rest day.

      Try it: click a square

      Make a split for each training day

      A split is just one workout day with its list of exercises, like Push Day or Leg Day.

      Go to Workouts, open the Splits tab, and press New split. Pick exercises from the library, or type your own.

      You make a split once and use it again and again. You need at least one split before you can log a workout.

      Example

      Push Day4 exercises
      Bench Press Incline Bench Press Dumbbell Shoulder Press Tricep Pushdown

      Track your lifts and watch them climb

      After training, open Workouts and the Progress tab. Pick a split and type only the weights or reps that changed.

      RepCord remembers your latest lift and your best (heaviest) lift, and draws a chart so you can see yourself getting stronger.

      You can use kg or lb. If you switch, your old numbers are converted for you.

      Example chart

      Bench Press: 60 kg → 75 kg

      Keep your streak alive

      A streak is how many days in a row you trained. If you skip a day, the count starts over, so it's a good reason to show up.

      The calendar page shows your current streak, your longest streak ever, and how many workouts you did this month.

      Common questions

      Why can't I save a workout?

      You need at least one split first. Go to Workouts, open the Splits tab, and make one. Then go back to your calendar.

      Can I change a workout after I save it?

      Yes. Click that day on the calendar to change the split or how hard it was, or to delete it. You can also delete workouts from Workouts, then History.

      Can I use pounds instead of kilograms?

      Yes. You choose kg or lb for each exercise. If you switch, your old numbers are converted so your chart stays correct.

      Will my workouts show up on my other devices?

      Yes. Your workouts are saved to your account, so just log in on any device.

      What counts as a streak?

      Days in a row with a saved workout. If you skip a full day, your current streak starts over. Your longest streak is always kept.
